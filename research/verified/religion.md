# Religion — verification pass, 4 October 2026

Scope: a targeted evidence audit, not comprehensive validation. “Confirmed” applies only to the narrow finding below. Unchecked old DOCUMENTED labels do not count as verification. Primary sources, later archival histories, and modern secondary corroboration are distinguished.

## R01 — confirmed
Prior claim: Catholic status quo protected in ceded Silesia

Berlin article VI protects the Catholic religious status quo alongside Protestant freedom of conscience and constrains sovereign interference. Treaty obligation is not proof of perfect enforcement.

Source: [BERLIN](https://documentsdedroitinternational.fr/ressources/TdP/1742-07-28-TraitedeBerlin.pdf), 28 July 1742, articles IV–VI and IX; printed pp. 742–745. primary treaty in Wenck collection; PDF downloaded and text read.

## R02 — confirmed
Prior claim: Clerical obligations contributed to Hungarian mobilisation

Act LXIII sections 13–16 specify obligations for named clerical categories, with exemptions and assessment rules. It does not establish papal permission or a monarchy-wide cash total.

Source: [H63](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100063.TV), 1741 Act LXIII, preamble and sections 1–4, 13–18. primary statute in published Hungarian translation.

## R03 — corrected
Prior claim: The Prague expulsion belongs to the 1741–42 opening

The decree is dated December 1744. It cannot be imported into 1741–42 as an event or proof of actual collaboration.

Source: [GHDI44](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/the-holy-roman-empire-1648-1815/ghdi:document-5366), December 1744 expulsion decree, opening paragraph. primary decree in translation.
