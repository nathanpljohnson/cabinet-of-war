# Economy — verification pass, 4 October 2026

Scope: a targeted evidence audit, not comprehensive validation. “Confirmed” applies only to the narrow finding below. Unchecked old DOCUMENTED labels do not count as verification. Primary sources, later archival histories, and modern secondary corroboration are distinguished.

## E01 — refuted
Prior claim: Hungary paid only in men and loyalty, never cash

Act LXIII funds infantry from the country’s tax fund, and section 18 specifies cash contributions by towns in lieu of some mounted obligations. Noble exemption is not national fiscal exemption.

Source: [H63](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100063.TV), 1741 Act LXIII, preamble and sections 1–4, 13–18. primary statute in published Hungarian translation.
Source: [H8](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100008.TV), 1741 Act VIII, full article. primary statute in published Hungarian translation.

## E02 — confirmed
Prior claim: Silesian revenues secured foreign debt transferred by peace

Berlin article IX assigns debt owed to English and Dutch subjects to Prussia, subject to the Dutch settlement provision, while the queen retains debt owed to Brabant creditors. No principal amount verified.

Source: [BERLIN](https://documentsdedroitinternational.fr/ressources/TdP/1742-07-28-TraitedeBerlin.pdf), 28 July 1742, articles IV–VI and IX; printed pp. 742–745. primary treaty in Wenck collection; PDF downloaded and text read.

## E05 — corrected
Prior claim: Occupation affects only next year’s budget; British subsidy never arrives when needed

These absolutes are unsupported design rules. Occupation may interrupt current collections and seize chests; subsidy authorisation, remittance and receipt must be tracked separately. Exact British payment dates and sums remain unverified here.
