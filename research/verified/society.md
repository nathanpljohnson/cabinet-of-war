# Society — verification pass, 4 October 2026

Scope: a targeted evidence audit, not comprehensive validation. “Confirmed” applies only to the narrow finding below. Unchecked old DOCUMENTED labels do not count as verification. Primary sources, later archival histories, and modern secondary corroboration are distinguished.

## S01 — confirmed
Prior claim: Hungarian noble tax privileges were reaffirmed

Act VIII protects the estates’ fiscal privileges. It does not show that all inhabitants or all forms of contribution were exempt.

Source: [H8](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100008.TV), 1741 Act VIII, full article. primary statute in published Hungarian translation.

## S02 — confirmed
Prior claim: 1741 infantry levy authorised

Act LXIII section 1 specifies 21,622 infantry in six new regiments; sections 1–2 specify funding and limits on replacement obligations. This is authorised establishment, not a verified muster or battlefield strength.

Source: [H63](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100063.TV), 1741 Act LXIII, preamble and sections 1–4, 13–18. primary statute in published Hungarian translation.

## S03 — corrected
Prior claim: 60,000 Hungarian troops actually provided

Replace the unsourced delivered total with the 21,622 authorised infantry levy, separately from noble and mounted obligations. Actual mobilisation and arrival remain unverified.

Source: [H63](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100063.TV), 1741 Act LXIII, preamble and sections 1–4, 13–18. primary statute in published Hungarian translation.
