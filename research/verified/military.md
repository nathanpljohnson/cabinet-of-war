# Military — verification pass, 4 October 2026

Scope: a targeted evidence audit, not comprehensive validation. “Confirmed” applies only to the narrow finding below. Unchecked old DOCUMENTED labels do not count as verification. Primary sources, later archival histories, and modern secondary corroboration are distinguished.

## M01 — corrected
Prior claim: 7,000 Austrians versus 100,000 Prussians invading Silesia

KA II counts 7,359 excluding Glatz against at least 26,000–27,000 invaders on Silesian soil in late December. KA I’s 99,446 is the whole Prussian army. The 7,359 includes garrisons and is not one field army.

Source: [KA1](https://archive.org/details/p1sterreichisc01aust), vol. I part 1, pp. 309–310, 381–382, 572–573. 1896 official history citing archives; secondary, not contemporary testimony.
Source: [KA2](https://archive.org/details/sterreichische02austuoft), vol. II, pp. 4–5, 58, opening of January 1741 chapter, 224–229, 245–246. official archival history; secondary with quoted returns.

## M02 — corrected
Prior claim: 1,719 men in October 1740

KA II says one regiment was in Silesia in October, but explicitly takes 1,719 from December strength lists. Do not label it an October return.

Source: [KA2](https://archive.org/details/sterreichische02austuoft), vol. II, pp. 4–5, 58, opening of January 1741 chapter, 224–229, 245–246. official archival history; secondary with quoted returns.

## M03 — confirmed
Prior claim: Mollwitz force estimates in the official history

KA II p. 229 reconstructs 15,788 Austrians from post-battle returns plus losses; p. 224 gives 21,600 Prussians. Preserve source and estimation status. These totals do not map directly to the demo’s counters.

Source: [KA2](https://archive.org/details/sterreichische02austuoft), vol. II, pp. 4–5, 58, opening of January 1741 chapter, 224–229, 245–246. official archival history; secondary with quoted returns.

## M04 — corrected
Prior claim: Austrian casualties imply a universal 29% rout threshold

KA II pp. 245–246 totals 4,551 Austrian personnel losses, including missing and prisoners as well as killed and wounded. Dividing by estimated strength describes one battle; it supplies no universal morale threshold.

Source: [KA2](https://archive.org/details/sterreichische02austuoft), vol. II, pp. 4–5, 58, opening of January 1741 chapter, 224–229, 245–246. official archival history; secondary with quoted returns.

## M05 — corrected
Prior claim: Wooden ramrods prove a sustained 3:2 firing ratio

KA I pp. 381–382 describes wooden ramrods and a December 1744 resolution for iron ones. Equipment difference alone establishes neither battlefield sustained rates nor a damage multiplier; universal delivery by that date is not shown.

Source: [KA1](https://archive.org/details/p1sterreichisc01aust), vol. I part 1, pp. 309–310, 381–382, 572–573. 1896 official history citing archives; secondary, not contemporary testimony.

## M06 — corrected
Prior claim: 1748 Principles document Frederick’s exact 1740 doctrine

The treatise was written in 1748 and circulated in 1753. Use as later reflection, not contemporary evidence that the novice king or every unit already behaved that way.

Source: [GHDI48](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/the-holy-roman-empire-1648-1815/ghdi:document-3582), editorial dating 1748/1753 and chapter 2. primary military treatise in translation, later than campaign.

## M07 — still unverified
Prior claim: Demo speed, fire, morale, vision, strength and casualty coefficients

The 1.55 fire and 1.18 strength multipliers, movement speeds, 15-minute/12-phase cap and fog ranges remain game assumptions. No source supports substituting precise new coefficients in this pass.


## Must not be stated as fact

Unverified figures, automatic political reactions, invented correspondence, and deterministic outcomes. Counterfactual effects remain design hypotheses even where their historical starting condition is confirmed.

## Recommended parameter treatment
