# Politics — verification pass, 4 October 2026

Scope: a targeted evidence audit, not comprehensive validation. “Confirmed” applies only to the narrow finding below. Unchecked old DOCUMENTED labels do not count as verification. Primary sources, later archival histories, and modern secondary corroboration are distinguished.

## P01 — corrected
Prior claim: Gotter instructions dated 17 November 1740

Document 192 is dated Berlin, 8 December 1740; it refers to earlier instructions to Borcke. Do not conflate those instructions with Gotter’s commission.

Source: [PC1](https://friedrich.uni-trier.de/de/politKorr/1/text/), documents 192 (8 December 1740), 227 (26 December 1740), 344 (13 April 1741). edited primary correspondence; full text opened.

## P02 — refuted
Prior claim: Frederick’s price could only rise; partial cession was unavailable

On 26 December Frederick authorized Gotter to indicate willingness to accept a substantial part rather than all of Silesia. This documents an offer, not sincerity or certainty that agreement would last.

Source: [PC1](https://friedrich.uni-trier.de/de/politKorr/1/text/), documents 192 (8 December 1740), 227 (26 December 1740), 344 (13 April 1741). edited primary correspondence; full text opened.

## P03 — corrected
Prior claim: Sardinia joined openly only in 1743

The Swiss National Museum dates the Austrian–Sardinian Convention of Turin to 1 February 1742. Correct the 1743-only claim; the original convention was not opened, so this is secondary corroboration.

Source: [TURIN](https://blog.nationalmuseum.ch/en/2023/05/the-encirclement-of-geneva-by-spanish-troops-in-1743/), paragraph beginning “The invasion prompted…”; 1 February 1742. museum historical account; secondary corroboration only.

## P04 — confirmed
Prior claim: Khevenhüller listed as Hofkriegsrat Vice-President in 1740/41

The official history gives that office and cites the 1740 and 1741 court calendars. Appointment month and presence in Vienna on 21 October remain unverified.

Source: [KA1](https://archive.org/details/p1sterreichisc01aust), vol. I part 1, pp. 309–310, 381–382, 572–573. 1896 official history citing archives; secondary, not contemporary testimony.

## P05 — still unverified
Prior claim: Court factions, exact Conference roster, all guarantors’ legal obligations, envoys and courier times

The pass does not establish these from dated records. Preserve as research hypotheses; no categorical legal or personal-motive conclusions.


## P06 — corrected
Prior claim: Mollwitz created the entire coalition from nothing

Document 344 refers to treaty negotiations already underway and a French letter dated 7 April. Victory may have affected commitment, but diplomacy did not begin only after 10 April.

Source: [PC1](https://friedrich.uni-trier.de/de/politKorr/1/text/), documents 192 (8 December 1740), 227 (26 December 1740), 344 (13 April 1741). edited primary correspondence; full text opened.

## Must not be stated as fact

Unverified figures, automatic political reactions, invented correspondence, and deterministic outcomes. Counterfactual effects remain design hypotheses even where their historical starting condition is confirmed.
