# 20 ideas to make the map UI better

Status: all 20 are now built (revision 8); some are first versions.

1. ✅ **Smooth zoom.** Whole-number zoom steps, tiles load only when the move ends, overlays pause while you move, nothing blends full-screen during a zoom.
2. ✅ **Level of detail.** Only places inside the view and large enough for the zoom get labels (capped at 100), instead of ~450 live labels.
3. ✅ **Three-level borders.** Bold dark state borders with a light halo, mid-weight crown-land lines, thin province lines, plus a tint per realm.
4. ✅ **Zone legend and facts.** Colour legend in the Layers panel; hovering a realm shows ruler, evidence label and courier days; clicking opens its card (map pass, 4 Oct).
5. 🔶 **Trade routes.** Casing, colour by kind (river, road, salt), animated caravans, goods in the tooltip; next: line weight by season/volume, click for a goods table.
6. 🔶 **Known movements, not fog of war.** Arrows with status (confirmed / reported / rumour), report date, source; next: age-fade and "last seen" ghosts.
7. ⬜ **Reach rings.** Select a place and show how far a courier, an army and a supply train get in 1, 3, 7 days along roads, rivers and terrain, so information delay is visible.
8. ⬜ **Report-age ghosting.** Old reports render faint and blurred; fresh ones are crisp, so "what I know" is visible at a glance.
9. ✅ **3D town blocks.** Eight cities (Prague, Vienna, Breslau, Pressburg, Linz, Munich, Dresden, Brünn) as extruded buildings with roofs, walls and shadows over painted streets; next: landmarks (castle, cathedral, bridges) as hand-modelled pieces.
10. ⬜ **Fortresses, magazines, bridges, fords.** Icon layer with garrison and supply notes; star-fort footprints when zoomed.
11. ⬜ **Season and weather.** Autumn / winter / mud / fog filters that also change movement notes from your staff.
12. ⬜ **Terrain lenses.** One-key switch between relief, forest and farmland, rivers and fords, "going" (dry/mud), supply.
13. ⬜ **Bookmarks and mini-map.** Hotkeys for Court, Frontier, Battle, plus an overview inset with your report limits drawn on it.
14. ⬜ **Timeline strip.** Dispatches, courier ETAs and scheduled events along the bottom; scrub to see the map as of a past date.
15. ⬜ **Proper military counters.** Period-style unit symbols that stack and fan out when you zoom, with formation icons instead of plain blocks.
16. ⬜ **Measure tool.** Drag a line and your staff reports days of march by arm (foot, horse, wagons) without you doing arithmetic.
17. ⬜ **Annotations.** Draw, pin and note on the map; notes are saved with the campaign and included in packets sent to Claude.
18. 🔶 **Evidence badges everywhere.** Everything on the map carries documented / inference / demo labels; next: a card on click for every route, border and movement.
19. ⬜ **Ambience.** Optional wind, bells, drums and cart sounds by zoom and place.
20. ⬜ **Readability and access.** Label halos tuned to the backdrop, colour-blind-safe palette option, reduced-motion mode, keyboard map navigation, scalable UI.
