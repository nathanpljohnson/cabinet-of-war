## Final audit

### A. Ten most important changes, ranked

1. **CHANGE: stop re-sending last turn's output in the packet.** Reports, gazette, whereabouts, decision, scene, hidden.decisions and hidden.fortune_used are cleared by the merge rules anyway, yet they made up about 14k of the 80k Round 2 packet. Send a one-line index of last turn's letter titles instead. Evidence: Round 2 packet sections; the closed scene appeared twice.
2. **CHANGE: make journeys real time.** Travel should put the player "on the road" until the adjudicated arrival, with letters reaching him at stages. Stop letting him stand in the destination at once, or back home before he left. Evidence: Glogau on 3 Nov (arrived 8th); Vienna on 12 Nov (arrived 14th); "in person" offered five days early. This undercuts "Presence" and "Absence costs something", the contract's best idea.
3. **CHANGE: send only live almanac threads.** Send full entries only for building/triggered threads and those due within ~30 days. List dormant ones as id + title only, or not at all. Evidence: 17 entries (14.7k) every turn, including papal neutrality and clerical contributions in November 1740; the 47-row thread list (4.1k) besides.
4. **REMOVE: demo material in the opening state.** The demo briefing, s0-demo scene, DEMO movements, "demo" cast claims and the Mollwitz preview are fed to the adjudicator as facts. Evidence: Round 1 state.
5. **CHANGE: "Let the day pass" stops on whereabouts and overheard days, not only post days.** Evidence: the audience day was skipped.
6. **CHANGE: fortune seed.** Salt it with something per campaign (record.chain head or a random value at campaign start). Evidence: `rollFortune(campaignId, turn)` with a fixed campaignId.
7. **CHANGE: battle view tied to the battle.** battleDemo needs its own date (season), terrain kind and unit list replaced whole per battle (objects with ids, or "replace" semantics). Otherwise drop `battle: true` for battles away from the current theatre. Evidence: Grocka in snow on the Mollwitz layout.
8. **ADD: a defined scene shape in the reply format** (or remove scenes and make audiences ordinary letters plus whereabouts). Rename either `scene` or `world.scene`. Evidence: I had to copy the demo; the audience was buried in the Notebook.
9. **CHANGE: player-facing letter chrome.** Drop the "T0-3" reference and the raw order text from letter headers (say "Your order of 21 October to Demeradt"). Show location names, not ids. Only label "in answer to" when the reply gave `re`; never guess. Evidence: the "Probably in answer to … Demeradt" header on the Queen's letter; "Concerning berlin".
10. **CHANGE: cap the append-only lists in the packet.** Send ledger, canon, dispatches and memory for the last N turns plus volume summaries, and keep the full record in the save. Evidence: after 3 turns ledger is 1.7k, canon 1.4k, dispatches 1.7k, memory 0.6k, all growing linearly (see F).

### B. Contract edits

- **Merge** the two source rules into one line: "Classify every claim as documented, inferred, disputed or campaign invention; never invent archival quotations." This replaces both "Classify claims as documented, inferred…" and "Concealed state must have documented, supported-inference…".
- **Cut** three of the four logistics statements. Keep only "The player never does logistics…" in Adjudication principles. Drop "## Staff work", the logistics sentence in "How orders work", and the last standing order (or keep the standing order and drop the principle).
- **Cut** the duplicate "## The seal" section (it appears twice, once with the value).
- **Rewrite** Fortune: "Use rolls in order for genuinely uncertain outcomes. Unused rolls are fine; say so in fortune_used." The current wording implies all eight must be used, which leads to invented uncertainty.
- **Rewrite** Pace / When to wake him: "End the turn on the first day something arrives or happens that the player must decide on, and no later than 14 days after the start."
- **Add** to Presence: "During a journey the player is on the road. In-person orders to people at the destination take effect on arrival. Letters that follow him arrive at the stage he has reached."
- **Add** a scene line: `"scene": {"id","title","setting","participants":[{"id","name"}],"lines":[{"who","text"}],"commitments":[…]}` — "use only when he asked for an audience".
- **Rewrite** Battles: "Use battle: true only for a battle in the current campaign. For one he asked about from the past, write an ordinary letter."
- **Cut** "at least Prussia, France, Bavaria, Britain, the rival". Replace with "every power or person whose choice this turn matters (usually 4–6)". In practice Britain and Saxony produced "nothing new" filler each round.
- **Rewrite** the gendered "he/his" to "the player" where it carries rules, if you want the game open to a player of either sex. Optional.

### D. Reply-format edits

- **Make optional:** gazette (two issues a turn was the most mechanical work), weather (give only days that change; carry forward otherwise), overheard.
- **Merge:** `hidden.dispatches` into `ledger`. Both record "what was sent and why"; dispatches was 1.7k after 3 turns and the player never sees it.
- **Rename:** `scene` → `audience` (vs `world.scene` tags).
- **Remove** from the skeleton: `decision` as a required feel. The page shows it, but the advisers' options were generic every time. Keep it optional for real forks.
- **Fix:** `battleDemo.units` — define as objects with id, or as "replace whole list"; the current "merge by id" doesn't match tuples.
- **Add:** an optional `player.route: [{date, place}]` so the page can place him on the road.

### E. Systems that never came up naturally

- **Table battle, army orders on the table, battleDemo.enemy, aftermath.** No battle is plausible before mid-December; the Mollwitz demo was dead weight in every packet. (I forced battle: true once.)
- **Judgement of sources (trust/doubt).** Never marked; the buttons exist on letters.
- **Mandate change / dismissal / endings / legacy / volumes.** Not reachable in three turns (volumes at turn 10).
- **Forged and intercepted letters, sketches beyond the seed.** Not used; nothing called for them.
- **Course chosen with the council / player map notes / table drafts.** Always "(none)".
- **Divergences.** One generic entry, never used.

These may be fine later, but they cost packet space every turn (speculative for the late ones).

### F. Long-campaign risks

- **Growth (measured, then projected).** Ledger, canon, dispatches and memory are append-only and sent whole. At about 5–6 entries a turn each, turn 50 would add roughly 100–120k chars, putting the packet near 200k chars (~50k tokens) before the reply. Projection from three turns, not tested.
- **Adjudicator context.** A 50k-token packet plus a 6k-token reply per turn will crowd out the reasoning. Expect thinner adjudication, skipped threads and more invented-but-unchecked facts after ~turn 20 unless the packet is pruned (speculative but likely).
- **Canon drift.** Canon is append-only with no ids. Contradictions between "settled facts" (e.g. dates I marked inference) will accumulate, and the continuity checker can only catch the simple ones.
- **Identical dice** across campaigns (code-confirmed).
- **Seed facts never researched.** The calendar anchors are "legacy … not independently checked"; the player's own office is "one source". These will be built on for 50 turns.
- **Chronicle at ≤300 words** is already at 257 by turn 3. It will hold until volume 1 at turn 10, then the volume summaries (200 words each) become the only long memory.

### G. Is it fun?

The court half is genuinely good. The banker lending half and asking why, Piccolomini saying nobody ever told him whether to defend Brieg, Neipperg freed while the player is away, and the fever at the worst moment all came from the contract's rules on presence, purse and the rival, not from my invention. It reads like history happening to you. What breaks it is the 3D layer not honouring those rules: you teleport to Glogau, Glogau is the Vienna palace, the day you wanted is skipped, the battle you asked about is the wrong battle in the wrong season. The single change that would most improve it is to make time and place real for the player: journeys that take days on a visible road, and a day-by-day clock that stops on every day something happens where he is. That one change carries the contract's best idea (you are in one place, and absence costs) into the thing the player actually sees.
