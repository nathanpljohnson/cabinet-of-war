### P01 — corrected

[PC1](https://friedrich.uni-trier.de/de/politKorr/1/text/): documents 192 (8 December 1740), 227 (26 December 1740), 344 (13 April 1741) (edited primary correspondence; full text opened).

### P02 — refuted

On 26 December Frederick authorized Gotter to indicate willingness to accept a substantial part rather than all of Silesia. This documents an offer, not sincerity or certainty that agreement would last.
[PC1](https://friedrich.uni-trier.de/de/politKorr/1/text/): documents 192 (8 December 1740), 227 (26 December 1740), 344 (13 April 1741) (edited primary correspondence; full text opened).

[TURIN](https://blog.nationalmuseum.ch/en/2023/05/the-encirclement-of-geneva-by-spanish-troops-in-1743/): paragraph beginning “The invasion prompted…”; 1 February 1742 (museum historical account; secondary corroboration only).

### E01 — refuted

Act LXIII funds infantry from the country’s tax fund, and section 18 specifies cash contributions by towns in lieu of some mounted obligations. Noble exemption is not national fiscal exemption.
[H63](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100063.TV): 1741 Act LXIII, preamble and sections 1–4, 13–18 (primary statute in published Hungarian translation).
[H8](https://net.jogtar.hu/ezer-ev-torveny?docid=74100008.TV): 1741 Act VIII, full article (primary statute in published Hungarian translation).

### E02 — confirmed

Berlin article IX assigns debt owed to English and Dutch subjects to Prussia, subject to the Dutch settlement provision, while the queen retains debt owed to Brabant creditors. No principal amount verified.
[BERLIN](https://documentsdedroitinternational.fr/ressources/TdP/1742-07-28-TraitedeBerlin.pdf): 28 July 1742, articles IV–VI and IX; printed pp. 742–745 (primary treaty in Wenck collection; PDF downloaded and text read).

### R01 — confirmed

Berlin article VI protects the Catholic religious status quo alongside Protestant freedom of conscience and constrains sovereign interference. Treaty obligation is not proof of perfect enforcement.
[BERLIN](https://documentsdedroitinternational.fr/ressources/TdP/1742-07-28-TraitedeBerlin.pdf): 28 July 1742, articles IV–VI and IX; printed pp. 742–745 (primary treaty in Wenck collection; PDF downloaded and text read).

### R02 — confirmed

Act LXIII sections 13–16 specify obligations for named clerical categories, with exemptions and assessment rules. It does not establish papal permission or a monarchy-wide cash total.
